Research Interests

I develop machine learning and statistical tools for analysis of single-cell multiomic data, with experience combining deep learning methods and biophysical modeling for deeper and scalable understanding of cellular systems. I am interested in transcriptional regulation, and the effects of genetic variants on this process, across cell types and contexts.
