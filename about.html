Hi! My name is Maria, and I'm a postdoctoral fellow at Stanford University working with Barbara Engelhardt on analysis methods for multimodal genomics data. I previously completed my Ph.D. with Lior Pachter at Caltech, where my research focused on using machine learning to understand the biophysical mechanisms underlying gene expression regulation using single-cell RNA-seq data. Please contact me if you have questions or are interested in collaborations; I am always thrilled to chat!

Outside of the noble scientific pursuit, I enjoy running in its various guises (from the trail to the road to the track), making and listening to music (choral, low-key, local bands, and much more), participating in improv comedy, reading, writing poetry, exploring craft fairs, wine-tasting, and really just the world in general.
